## What the controlled difference means

Both arms have 6,135 parameters, the same raw receiver skip, actual eight-neighbor
indices, masks and relative geometry, and matched seeds/orders. The control
permutes only sender features. It still sees actual neighboring relative XYZ,
range and modality descriptors. Therefore this is a sender-to-edge alignment
test, not a graph-versus-pointwise comparison or a rejection of all relational
information. The permutation may substitute a receiver's content on one edge.

The graph uses the authenticated public 132-by-21 token contract. Nominal ToF
centers and Radar's zero-height placeholder select neighbors; they are not
native hit coordinates. No RGB pixels, native object IDs, family, scene, fold,
label or baseline alert enters inference. Radar is context only. Raw packets,
range envelopes, MERGED ambiguity and outside-RGB support are never modified.

Known positives and negatives receive equal total fitting loss mass, computed
from each fold's training records only. UNKNOWN gets no loss. Both arms use
AdamW .001, weight decay .0001, batch16, 120 epochs and the final checkpoint,
720 updates per fold/arm. There is no threshold, epoch or model selection.
All six models and the out-of-fold predictions seal before validation reduction.
