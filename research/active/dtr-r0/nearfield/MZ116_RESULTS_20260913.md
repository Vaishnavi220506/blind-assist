## Why the merged-ToF problem is larger

All429 returned SIM_MERGED targets have a single real actor:370 are one native
planar face and59 span multiple faces of that actor. None has multiple actors,
and none even lies in a private zone containing multiple real actors. All spans
are0.100–0.327m;399 have one histogram maximum,30 multiple maxima. Thus a merged
status often describes slant-depth variation across an ordinary surface.

The32 merged-supported FP frames have264 supporting merged tuples, including
205 oblique single offroute-wall faces across31frames,57 multi-face wall tuples
and2 multi-face suspended-head tuples. The full.02–4m fallback is therefore a
major nuisance source. It is an explicit uncertainty policy, not evidence that
those nearby positions are measured obstacles.

An evaluator-only exact returned-contributor corridor oracle, replacing only
merged supports and preserving all other MZ115 candidate evidence, yields
**141TP/69FP/3FN→141/40/3**.29FP disappear, no TP is lost. Three other merged-FP
frames remain supported by Radar or valid ToF. This diagnoses potential on this
specific source; it is not achievable algorithm performance, and it excludes
unsampled surfaces and unreturned weak echoes.

Critically, the source provides **zero** returned multi-actor merged examples.
It cannot validate a proposed rule for close-weak-object plus far-wall mixtures.
Fitting a plane to these mostly single-face cases alone would leave that central
failure condition untested.

## Concrete information counterexample

[mz116_merged_collision.py](mz116_merged_collision.py) constructs two valid private
nine-ray configurations through the unchanged MZ115 reducer. Both report one
3.04m return, sigma.04m, signal.04 and SIM_MERGED, numerically equal within1e-12.
Both have weighted mean3.045m and obey latent reflectance bounds[0,1]. In one,
all sampled points miss the corridor; in the other, one2.55m contributor lies
inside it (lateral.2914m versus the first case's.3428m at that ray).

These are discrete sample configurations, not rendered continuous walls. RGB,
Radar and history are not claimed identical. The counterexample establishes a
ToF-tuple information limit: replacing mixed uncertainty with the mean, or with
an unproved narrow interval, cannot by itself certify absence of a nearer hit.
The possible escape is additional geometric/visual evidence or a richer measured
distribution, followed by actual multi-actor mixture tests—not access to private
sample identities or changed ground-truth labels.
