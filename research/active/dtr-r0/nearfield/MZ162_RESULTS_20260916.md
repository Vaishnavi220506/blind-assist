## Method and evidence limits

Each saved native hit resolves to actor+axis-aligned face only when exactly
one face is within1e-5m. Membership is the union of the resolved face set at
the saved-AABB first hit; multiple owners are not collapsed to one identity.
Incomplete sets mask their whole query label, rather than creating false
negative pixels. Pixel slant relative to the returned noisy aggregate is a
per-pixel diagnostic, never one slot-wide depth correction.

These are analytic first hits of native actor AABBs under a source-commanded
camera pose. They are not rendered segmentation/depth or independent camera
readback. Unsaved global environment may affect actual visibility; no source
geometry is inserted to fill it. Noncolliding texture tiles can sit1mm ahead
of the native faces. Missing/ambiguous references remain UNKNOWN. Consequently
the coverage bound applies to this declared saved-face relation, not a complete
physical or sensor-information ceiling.

All192 TRAIN records, including nominal scene3, were inspected for annotation
opportunity. A later scene3 fitting holdout would still be consumed Development,
not untouched design validation. No original dev/test record or RGB was decoded
by the audit. Earlier MZ161 saved visualizations were inspected separately for
design context; they were not new data or training observations.

This is an exploratory decision, not pre-parse preregistered confirmation.
