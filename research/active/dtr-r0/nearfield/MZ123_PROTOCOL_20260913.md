## Readout and requirements

Use the same seven development requirements on this fixed point: frame recall at least MZ116 minus 2 percentage points; at least 30% reduction in FP frames and false-alert bin duration; retain every baseline-detected positive event; maximum detected first-alert delay at most baseline +0.25 s; HEAD and rod true-cell recalls at least 90%. No positive denominator or zero baseline FP makes the corresponding reduction requirement not established. Report all checks, not just F1.

Retain overall/family/stress frame counts, events, missed episodes, onset delays, lost baseline TP, added/removed FP and all 45-cell TP/FP/FN/precision/IoU. MZ116 supplies alert comparison only, since it has no comparable 45-cell output. Report suspended-HEAD frame continuity and per-episode results so event hits cannot hide long intervals of missed frames. Report paired correct spatial-answer changes; a correct alert does not establish correct echo identity.
