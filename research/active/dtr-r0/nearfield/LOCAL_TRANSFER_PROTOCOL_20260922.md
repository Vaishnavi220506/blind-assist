# LOCAL frozen paired transfer: appearance stability and lateral sensitivity

2026-09-22. Authorized EXPLORE after the inherited-mechanism batch. One new
controlled Development capture and one fixed-model evaluation. No fitting,
threshold selection, feature revision, alternative appearance schedule or
automatic successor. The old spatial result and its failed system gate stay fixed.

Question: does the retained query-local representation supply useful boundary
coverage on new geometries, remain stable when appearance changes, and respond
to a lateral geometry intervention? The weakest premise is that the learned
local RGB/support package reflects transferable spatial evidence. Regional
range hypotheses do not identify which pixels or object generated a return.

The preparation diagnostic compares old saved 30 LOCAL-added TP, two added FP,
and one RAW-only TP within the full 576-frame denominator. It may describe
score/support/appearance differences; it cannot select a new cutoff or establish
separability from two negative examples. Its findings are explanatory hypotheses,
not a rule that changes the frozen predictor. Its two BODY-OUTSIDE false alerts
are actually won by HEAD-centre; their local possible-band color summaries
coincide with warm backdrop pixels. This motivates a background-only intervention
before new capture, without claiming that those two examples establish causality.

## Fixed decisions

Data admission for the relevant contrast requires all 576 frames/labels, all
288 appearance pairs, exact public ToF equality and full-extent label equality
within appearance pairs, and a genuine RGB change. Every pair must differ in
RGB; mean normalized RGB L1 over pairs must be at least 1/255. Save all per-pair
deltas and source geometry/native checks. Missing/inconsistent evidence makes
that contrast NOT_EVALUABLE; do not discard rows or manufacture a model failure.

Three separate requirements determine whether this component transfers:

1. **Task transfer.** In each appearance separately, LOCAL versus RAW union
   recall gain >=5 percentage points, FP and false segments each at most +1,
   net TP gain in at least four of eight base groups, and BODY/HEAD recall no
   more than 5 points below RAW. A TP/events/onset are preserved. The combined
   paired whole-base-group recall-gain bootstrap lower bound must exceed zero
   (1000 resamples, seed 202609225, both appearances stay in each sampled group).
2. **Appearance stability.** Changed versus base LOCAL recall drops no more
   than 5 points; FP and false segments each increase by at most one. There
   must be at least eight LOCAL-added TP over A in base; changed must retain
   >=75% of those same paired rescued identities. Fewer than eight means the
   rescue-stability clause lacks opportunity, not a positive stability result.
3. **Geometry sensitivity.** Separately in each appearance, LOCAL's frame score
   ranks BOUNDARY above paired OUTSIDE in >=75% of samples whose BOUNDARY truth
   is positive (64 planned pairs per appearance). Strict ties are not successes.
   This diagnostic ranking cannot substitute for the fixed-threshold task costs.

All three passing retains a controlled-transfer COMPONENT. A valid failure is
a scoped negative transfer/stability result, retaining the old component's
old-source value. Insufficient evidence/opportunity is reported explicitly.
Additionally report the original strict system criterion over all 576 frames:
LOCAL versus A recall +10 points, at most +2 FP/+1 false segment, no A TP/event
or onset losses. Its budget is unchanged. Passing would concern this controlled
source only and does not authorize default App or user/safety claims.
