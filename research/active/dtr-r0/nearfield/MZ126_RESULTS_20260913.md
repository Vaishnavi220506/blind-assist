**Central narrowing substantially reduces nuisance but sacrifices boundary
coverage and timeliness.** The half-width target leaves MZ116 unchanged; the
quarter-width target gives 130 TP / 58 FP / 14 FN versus 139 / 116 / 5, with
all nine newly missed frames in boundary stress. Ordinary BODY/HEAD/rod frame
recall is preserved. Keep this as a measured Development tradeoff and zone-selection
diagnostic component; retain MZ116 as the default baseline.

## Actual sensor geometry

The source has 8x8 zones with 45-degree horizontal ToF coverage. RGB width is
640 pixels. Both narrowed arms retain all eight vertical rows. The selection
uses complete projected zone footprints; boundary-crossing zones are discarded,
not cropped and not assigned to their center rays.

| Requested image width | Image band x | ToF zones kept | Actual ToF x span | Actual image width covered |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Full / unchanged | 0–640 | 64 (8 columns) | 130.70–509.30 | 59.16% |
| Central half | 160–480 | 48 (6 columns) | 181.37–458.63 | 43.32% |
| Central quarter | 240–400 | 16 (2 columns) | 274.99–365.01 | 14.07% |

Thus the quarter-width target tests a stricter **14.07% actual horizontal
coverage**, not a continuously realizable exact 25% crop. The middle four columns
cross the requested boundaries and were not included. No alternative overlap or
center-zone rule was added after the outcome. This also shows why reducing image
width to half is not halving the original ToF field of view.

## What was removed, beyond final alert counts

The half target discards 239 returned records, including 83 previously active
ToF supports. Other supports preserve every final alert. None of those removed
records contains native hazardous-actor or actual corridor-hit samples.

The quarter target discards 2,634 records, including 1,001 previously active
supports. Removed records contain 3,012 hazardous-actor contributing samples and
552 actual corridor-hit samples. Hazardous-actor evidence is discarded in 66
frames, 57 of which still alert through remaining support. These are contributing
sample records, not unique objects or additional missed events. The native actor
label and actual hit-in-corridor label are distinct; both are evaluator-only.

This is intentional loss of measured coverage, unlike MZ125's retained-contributor
localization correction. BODY/HEAD frame retention cannot certify that their
original ToF coverage remains intact. Original Radar/guard/yaw and all retained
zone footprints, range bounds and coarse XYZ remain identical; positive RGB/ToF
association is recomputed after selection. No range is relabeled into the center.
