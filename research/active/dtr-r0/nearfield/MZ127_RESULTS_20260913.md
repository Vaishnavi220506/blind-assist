## Why the gains do not add

Relative to full-width MZ125, the combination **adds five HEAD FP and removes
two BODY FP**, a net increase of three FP. The affected IDs are:

- Restored HEAD alarms: `mz123_suspended_head_pair1_out_03`, `_04`, `_06`, `_07`, `_11`.
- Removed BODY alarms: `mz123_substantial_body_pair1_out_00`, `_01`.

In those five HEAD frames the recovered RGB box is still available, but only
one valid ToF return (zone18) remains in its group after column selection.
MZ115's unchanged positive association requires at least two zones. The retained
return therefore falls back from an associated image region to
`WHOLE_ZONE_UNRESOLVED`; that broader region overlaps the corridor and alerts.

Peripheral measurements thus served two roles: they could trigger nuisance
alarms, but also helped associate the remaining range with the correct image
region. Removing them before association can degrade localization without any
change to RGB pixels or boxes. Full-context association followed by a narrower
alert-use policy would be a different, untested mechanism; it was not added here.

Full-width MZ125 has lower false-frame count/duration and higher precision;
combined has two fewer false segments. This is not strict dominance across all
reported nuisance measures. Sampled false segments are not real notification
frequency or a demonstrated user-experience benefit.

ToF supports. The discarded records contain90 native hazardous-actor contributor
samples across7 frames and zero actual corridor-hit samples. Actor membership is
not equivalent to the sampled point being inside the corridor. Zero removed
corridor hits in this constructed batch cannot establish universal edge coverage,
and preserved final recall does not prove that no useful ToF evidence was removed.

equal their originals; Radar/guard/yaw are unchanged. Association is recomputed
using the selected measurements, so this is not filtering cached final alerts.
Native lineage is evaluator-only and does not select the four columns or boxes.

Independent native audit passes. For every retained return with changed ROI/XYZ,
baseline-to-four-original covers2 returns/9 contributor samples, full-MZ125-to-

has zero candidate ROI exclusions, XYZ exclusions or newly excluded native samples,
including hazardous-actor/actual-corridor samples in still-alerting frames. These
counts exclude the989 deliberately discarded returns described above.
