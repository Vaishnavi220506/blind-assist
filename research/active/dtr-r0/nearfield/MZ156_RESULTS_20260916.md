## Method and authority

One RGB, original 8x8 two-return ToF, Radar and IMU remain unchanged. No capture,
model fit, threshold selection, scan change, original MZ136/MZ146 decoding or
protected test access occurs. Reuse both sealed MZ155 predictor outputs and all
288 consumed observations, 144 per arm, each with 72 positive and 72 negative
frames. The MZ155 source, public observations, prediction bindings and image
hashes are authenticated without rerunning a model or decoding RGB.

A current witness requires the entire angular zone and nominal slant-range
interval to lie inside the corridor. Bounds include 3 sigma, .10 m aggregation
span and .01 m quantization, plus accumulated public-IMU yaw uncertainty and
pitch uncertainty. All valid slots are eligible; no RGB clipping, source target
identity or native pose enters prediction. These are nominal simulator-contract
intervals, not hard Gaussian bounds, simultaneous confidence guarantees or
hardware calibration. Public camera offsets are applied once.

Each witness is stored in its original body coordinates. In the explicitly known
stationary body-position, camera-position and world condition, it lasts at most
1.25 s across six contiguous .25 s samples. Rig yaw may change. Missing ToF alone
cannot erase a valid historical positive. Broken IMU/cadence/calibration clears
and disables this branch until a new forward-aligned episode. It is disabled by
default; the experiment explicitly supplies the static-world condition. There
is no online scene-motion detector, and stopping walking does not establish
that other objects are static.

Current and prefix witnesses are separately ORed with unchanged MZ129. This
preserves its alerts within each observation arm and cannot reduce that arm's
false positives. MZ155's failed fixed recipe and MZ152's original failure remain
unchanged; this replay tests a distinct conditional temporal mechanism.
