# MZ123 — the frozen early development gain did not survive this new controlled cohort

The MZ122 same-round **early** checkpoint at **0.58** completed a new, frozen
288-frame comparison. It does not meet the joint alert/spatial requirements.
MZ116 remains the baseline. This does not erase early's MZ122 development result
or the local-fusion arm's measured spatial-envelope improvement.

Early removes 31 baseline false-positive frames but adds 19, for a net reduction
of 12/116 = **10.34%**, below the frozen 30% requirement. It loses 20 baseline
true-positive frames and gains three, for a net loss of 17. Fewer false-alert
segments therefore coexist with worse frame recall and F1. UNKNOWN is the
non-alert state, not a claim that space is clear.

Six of seven checks fail: frame recall, FP reduction, false-duration reduction,
event retention, HEAD cell recall and rod cell recall. Only the maximum delay
**among detected events** passes. That statistic excludes the entirely missed
event and cannot establish acceptable onset performance on all events.

## Spatial and paired answers

At the unchanged 0.58 threshold, full 45-cell results are TP 1,148, FP 2,403,
FN 448: precision **32.33%**, recall **71.93%**, IoU **28.71%**. HEAD cells are
111/149 (**74.50%**) recalled, with 325 false occupied cells. Rod true core cells
are 60/72 (**83.33%**) recalled. Correct frame alarms therefore do not establish
adequate spatial occupancy; MZ116 has no comparable 45-cell output.

Across 144 aligned paired-frame comparisons, both opposite alert answers are
correct in **24/144 for MZ116** and **21/144 for early**. By family, HEAD improves
10/36 to 17/36, rod/far-wall falls 9/36 to 0/36, substantial body falls 4/36 to
0/36, and stress improves 1/36 to 4/36. Preserve those localized gains alongside
the entirely missed HEAD event. Early alerts on both members of every rod/wall
and substantial-body pair. On the 582 cells whose native occupancy changes,
early answers both members correctly for 143, independently of alert correctness.
Within-pair geometry/appearance/camera motion are matched but episode sensor
noise differs. This is an answer-change check, not a pure causal sensor-shuffle
or physical echo-assignment experiment.

## Decision and next boundary

Terminal: `FRESH_CONTROLLED_JOINT_REQUIREMENTS_NOT_MET`. Intended inheritance:
`NEGATIVE_CONTROL` for **this fixed MZ122 early checkpoint at 0.58 as a fresh
controlled joint-alert challenger**. Preserve the successful consumed development
point and MZ122's separate local-fusion spatial findings. Neither is promoted
into a reliable alert mainline by this result; neither learned fusion as a whole
nor sensor information sufficiency is disproved.

The new observations justify studying suspended-HEAD retention and positional
in/out discrimination when a later experiment is authorized. They do not yet
identify loss misalignment, visual shortcuts, insufficient data, capacity or
specific sensor input as the cause. No new loss fit, threshold rescue, CNH,
temporal model or data-expansion campaign follows automatically.
