# MZ125: observable evidence behind false alerts and one correction contrast

The user authorized the focused next action after MZ124: identify observable
differences between false alerts and real obstacles, then test one concrete
correction mechanism. This does not authorize another broad training campaign.

## Evidence question and scope

Use all 288 already consumed MZ123 frames, original MZ116 and early outputs,
the original raw RGB/ToF/Radar/IMU, and native evaluator/provenance for audits
only. No capture, training, early threshold change, MZ123 rerun-as-confirmation,
or source relabeling. All findings are constructed Development diagnostics.

First attribute every false and true alert to current ToF/Radar/guard support
and returned sensor lineage. A frame TP is not proof that the alert refers to
the hazardous actor. Quantized packet collisions may establish ambiguity;
absence of exact collisions in noisy data does not establish useful separability.
Matched opposite scenes have independent sensor noise, so raw differences are
not automatically causal information about obstacle position.

## Concrete hypothesis and one candidate

Inspected RGB examples show a bright suspended obstacle or large offroute body
against smooth background, while inherited proposal lists are missing or describe
large background regions. Hypothesis: a pixel-only local-background residual can
recover useful angular extent, letting existing positive multi-zone ToF association
distinguish some offroute returns without changing range bounds or Radar policy.

Fit a robust quadratic horizontal background per image row, six fixed asymmetric
reweighting iterations. Positive residual must exceed both 8 gray levels and four
robust residual scales. Close the binary mask with a fixed 3x3 kernel, retain
components with at least 12 pixels and width below 80% of image width, without
the old 95%-height exclusion. No labels, frame IDs, camera source roles, actor
names, or known reflectance enter extraction. This deliberately tests a controlled
contrast assumption, not a natural-image detector or proof of complete visibility.

The candidate feeds positive recovered boxes to the unchanged MZ115 multi-zone
signal/coverage association. If none are recovered, retain the original proposals.
All range/noise bounds, minimum two-zone grouping, association cosine/margin,
merged-return treatment and core corridor stay fixed. Recompute ToF allocation;
retain original MZ116 Radar/guard support and certain coarse ToF support. No
common-plane assumption or merged-mean depth contraction from MZ118 is reused.
This differs from MZ118's size-filter restoration: the changed pixel foreground
extraction is the tested input, and the plane fitting path is absent.

Before truth scoring, reproduce all original cached ToF allocations and original
alert flags from observable inputs, then serialize candidate output and hashes.

baseline frame identities. Audit every newly contracted ToF region against
