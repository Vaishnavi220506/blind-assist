# MZ118: border-region recovery exposes a false common-plane assumption

The new auxiliary RGB branch recovers geometric coverage, but its interval-plane
readout is **not retained for obstacle localization**. It removes three old-panel
false alerts while excluding genuine near-obstacle contributors on 44 mixed-source
frames. Every affected frame still alerts. Aggregate recall therefore hides the
localization failure that this mainline must resolve.

[Protocol](MZ118_PROTOCOL_20260913.md), EXPLORE, 480 consumed constructed frames:
all MZ115 and MZ117 inputs, unchanged hypothetical finite-zone sensor model. Prior
outcomes were known. One frozen paired CPU replay; no source regeneration, tuning
or fresh-confirmation claim. Keep MZ116 as the runnable research challenger.

three near-rod hits at slant ranges 3.24552, 3.26526, 3.28879 m (forward x3.20 m,

The plane solver is satisfying its anchor constraints. The failed assumption is
that an eligible RGB region and compatible merged mean make the mixed target part
of that same plane. More precise conditional geometry cannot establish missing
foreground identity. This is the user-requested near-rod / wall competition case,
and it prevents promotion despite unchanged frame recall.
