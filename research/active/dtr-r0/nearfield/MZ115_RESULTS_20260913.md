# MZ115: finite ToF footprints and RGB allocation

The fixed RGB allocation makes a small, contributor-audited spatial improvement,
but does not establish a nuisance breakthrough or an alert replacement. On the
same newly captured 240-frame public measurements, primary3.6m results change
141TP/71FP/3FN to141/69/3. All14 positive events still alert at their first sampled
positive bin. False duration falls17.75→17.25s, but false segments rise18→19:
shorter occupied intervals can fragment into more reminders. At2.6m a TP is lost
and maximum first-alert delay rises2→2.25s. No domination of the full curve.

## What changed and what was compared

[Frozen protocol](MZ115_PROTOCOL_20260913.md). The new simulator reports finite
zone/multiple-return tuples, not a single center-ray distance. Each8×8 zone has
3×3 private angular probes, hypothetical independent reflectance/signal,
strongest2 detected peaks, merging/status and range noise. RGB/Radar/IMU remain
the same architecture. The old center-ray ToF exists only in evaluator controls.
This is sparse quadrature with a hypothetical signal model, not a continuous
physical beam, ST firmware reproduction, hardware thin-pole result or RF test.

The algorithm contrast uses identical public tuples in both arms: full-footprint
possible occupancy versus conditional RGB allocation by positive signal shape.
Both share the same independent RGB+Radar filtered-plane/short-flow branch.
There is no mandatory ToF hit for a Radar association. RGB narrows only a valid
component's intersecting zone ROI, never paints its range over the whole image
box. Ambiguous and merged returns remain full footprints; merged depth keeps
the entire sensing-range uncertainty. Missing returns remain UNKNOWN, and
unmatched Radar retains HEIGHT_UNKNOWN. Model changes are not algorithm gains.

## Disposition

Keep the finite-zone interface, contributor audit and spatial allocation as
`COMPONENT_OR_CHALLENGER` within constructed Development. No default alert
promotion. Carry the2.6m lost-TP/delay and reminder-fragmentation counterexamples;
do not tune consumed source thresholds or claim that shape cosine proves identity.
The previously retained MZ113 component and its original center-ray measurements
remain a separate authenticated comparison history, not a numerical comparator
for this changed sensor source. The larger optimization goal remains active.

Evidence is under `artifacts.local/work/mz115-zonal-allocation-20260913/`:
`source`, `capture-v1`, `analysis-v1`, `audit-v1`; all240 frames are now consumed
Development. Preserve the source freeze, raw/evaluator separation and receipts.
`diagnostic-v1` retains the executable sealed-evidence interpretation and exact
remaining-support/FN/boundary examples. The next information work must address
merged-surface depth ambiguity and Radar/visual proposal failures; merely making
valid-return boxes tighter cannot resolve these independent support burdens.

Structured registration was attempted and failed on the existing ledger line303
input-fingerprint mismatch. `pending-registration.json` preserves the exact
command/error and intended challenger inheritance. No successful registration,
structured terminal assignment or knowledge acceptance is claimed. Unrelated
ledger rows were not changed; this gap does not block scoped code delivery.
