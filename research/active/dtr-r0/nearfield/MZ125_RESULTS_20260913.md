# MZ125: a concrete RGB angular-extent correction reduces 11 nuisance frames

**Retain a scoped Development component, keep MZ116 as the baseline.** On all
288 consumed MZ123 frames, one pixel-only foreground change followed by unchanged
ToF association reduces FP from 116 to 105 while preserving 139 TP, 5 FN, all
15 events, .25 s maximum first-alert delay and 27 false segments. The native
contributor audit finds no hidden deletion of near-obstacle evidence.

This is a useful narrow result, not a general performance breakthrough. All 11
removed FP belong to two offroute suspended-HEAD episodes. BODY, rod/farwall and
boundary-stress alert outcomes remain unchanged. No new capture or training ran.

## What the evidence audit changed

The 116 incumbent FP comprise 80 ToF-only, 25 joint Radar/ToF and 11 Radar-only
frames. But 15 of the 139 TP also rely solely on ToF, so removing the entire
branch would lose useful coverage. Merged does not imply multiple objects:
223/230 active merged FP returns come from a single native actor.

Three rod TP frames are more subtle: their only active support comes from the
farwall rather than the hazardous rod. They are frame-level true positives,
not correctly identified near-obstacle evidence. All three remain unchanged in
this trial. Neither their presence nor 288 unique noisy packet signatures proves
that the existing pipeline has reliable object attribution or that every pair is
informatively separable. Native source identities remain evaluator-only.

## One input change with the rest of the correction fixed

The recovered foreground comes from robust horizontal background subtraction
on each RGB image row: six fixed quadratic reweighting iterations, residual
above 8 gray levels and four robust scales, 3x3 closing, components of at least
12 pixels and width below 80% of the image. Unlike the old frontend, tall
clipped components are not discarded solely for exceeding 95% image height.
This has an explicit bright-object/smooth-background assumption.

The new proposals feed only MZ115's original positive multi-zone ToF association.
Grouping, range uncertainty, coverage/cosine/margin requirements, certain coarse
support and merged treatment stay fixed. Original Radar, guard and causal yaw
remain byte-for-byte equivalent as data; absent new foreground falls back to the
old proposal list. No image absence establishes clearance. No MZ118 common-plane
fit, merged range contraction, model weights or alert threshold is introduced.

All 288 old ToF allocations and baseline alert flags are reproduced before
candidate scoring. Candidate outputs are saved before the evaluator frame labels
are read. The full RGB archive is hash-checked against the original capture
receipt; all ranges and coarse spatial bounds are unchanged.

The candidate recovers positive components in 250 frames, compared with 161
frames with any old proposal. Counts alone do not establish good segmentation:
38 frames still fall back, and a viewed substantial-body example has an empty
new mask. Whole-image photometric/scene generalization was not tested.

## Contributor retention, beyond frame recall

The independent audit inspects all 3,954 ToF returns. In 114 frames, 446 returned
regions change ROI/XYZ geometry; all **1,678** native contributing samples remain
inside the new ROI and localized XYZ. These include 866 samples belonging to
hazardous actors and 291 actual points inside the corridor. There are zero new
ROI/XYZ exclusions, including in frames whose final alert remains true.

All 266 merged returns retain their original full .02–4 m range and whole-zone
ROI. Sixteen valid ToF supports disappear across 14 negative HEAD frames;
three still alert through unchanged Radar, leaving the 11 FP reduction. All
32 native samples contributing to those 16 returns remain contained. Their
associated new boxes overlap the native actor's projected bounds with IoU
.918–.974, an evaluator-only agreement check. No runtime exact echo identity,
universal source completeness or natural-image localization is established.

This differs from MZ118's failed plane contraction: the observed benefit here
comes from angular extent, with range untouched and all audited native samples
retained. It also differs from MZ124's null-preserving generic union: this
candidate uses positive RGB/ToF association evidence while preserving the
incumbent Radar policy. It does not claim that all hypothetical raw Radar
working-interval possibilities are eliminated.

## Decision, reproduction and completion

Terminal: `MZ125_CONSUMED_ANGULAR_CORRECTION_GAIN`. Intended inheritance:
`COMPONENT_OR_CHALLENGER / COMPONENT` for pixel foreground extent plus the
unchanged positive ToF association on this constructed contrast. Keep MZ116
runnable and authoritative as the baseline; do not promote the candidate to a
new default or describe consumed evidence as fresh confirmation.

The result establishes that useful RGB location information was available and
underused in these two nuisance episodes. It does not resolve BODY, rod/farwall,
boundary ambiguity, the three incidental rod TP, or general learned occupancy.
No automatic new fit, threshold sweep or capture follows this bounded trial.

Four focused tests pass. Independent saved-result audits reproduce frame/event
counts, paired results, all old and new allocations, and every changed native
contributor. CPU replay took 21.835 s for 288 frames (about 75.8 ms/frame), including
image hashing, input checks, extraction, allocation and mask writes. The separate
foreground probe was about 82 ms; these are different scopes, not Android or
sensor-to-alert latency. Backend is NumPy/OpenCV CPU with no equivalent
implemented GPU path, recorded as `GPU_BACKEND_UNAVAILABLE`.

check was corrected. The aborted `correction-v1` and successful `correction-v1r1`
are both retained, with no experimental parameter change or outcome rescue.
