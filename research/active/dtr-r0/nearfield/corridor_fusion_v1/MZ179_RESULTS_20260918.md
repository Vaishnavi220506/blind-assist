## Decision

H1 is positive only for **descriptive history separability**. Added physical
information beyond current-range and model-score controls is unproven. H2 lacks
the proposed dropout in this subset. H3's strong assertion of no separability
is false for these selected six rows; its broader information-theoretic version
cannot be adjudicated by a finite feature list. Stop this round without model
training or a ledger. A later separately authorized probe would need matched
current evidence and more scene groups to identify a temporal contribution;
do not promote a distance threshold selected on these six cases.
