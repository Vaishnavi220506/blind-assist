## Context preservation, distinct from soft weighting

The binary control was specified before scoring, with weights[0,0,1,1,1,1,0,0],
the same threshold and certain/Radar protection. All64zones still participate
in MZ125 allocation, including the peripheral zones that resolve associations.

It removes exactly two BODY false frames from full MZ125:
`mz123_substantial_body_pair1_out_00` and `_01`. BODY FP32becomes30; HEAD remains
13FP, rod25FP, stress35FP. Baseline class recalls remain BODY36/36, HEAD33/36,
rod34/36 and stress36/36. The total13FP reduction versus MZ116 is11.21%, with
precision+2.93percentage points. The incremental gain over full MZ125 is only2FP.

MZ127's four-column-before-association combination had108FP. This control has
103FP because it retains the five HEAD exclusions lost when zone17 was removed
before association with zone18. Thus preserving context explains a five-frame
interaction on these cases. It does not demonstrate an advantage of continuous
weights: the binary control is better than the tested soft profile here.

The certain-coarse override remains active independently of zone weight. This is
therefore a binary alert-use control with an explicit evidence-preserving override,
not a claim that outer zones can never trigger. No scan of other weights or
thresholds was added after finding the null combined-soft result.

## Evidence and verification

arms reference these sealed allocations; no zone, native record, range, angular
footprint, association, ROI or XYZ geometry is deleted or changed. Decision
influence is reduced, which is different from preserving equal warning coverage.
