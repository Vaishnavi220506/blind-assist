# MZ155: fixed-budget angular sampling of a stationary world

EXPLORE Development, 2026-09-16. MZ153/154 do not recover current-return-absent
rod surfaces. Their source audit also finds target/camera lateral co-motion and
small motion. Test a distinct observation mechanism: predetermined pure yaw
rotation changes which parts of a static world fall in original regional ToF
zones. It is angular sampling diversity, not translation parallax or a rescue
of the closed pretrained-depth recipe. No pretrained depth inference here.

## Source and comparison frozen before capture

Use seed155016 of the unchanged MZ136 geometry generator, first three scene
configurations in each of its four families. Only new generated geometry is
used; no original raw TRAIN/dev/test or MZ146 cohort is decoded. Take each pair's
initial target lateral position relative to the body, set body/camera lateral
origin to zero, and keep all objects and body position fixed for six frames.
Boundary members now stay just inside/outside throughout; this does not test
walking onset, crossing or exit. Each family has three inside and three outside
cases in each observation arm: 12 groups,24 cases,48 episodes,288 frames total.

The passive arm has yaw0. The scan arm uses yaw=(45/8)/2*sin(2*pi*i/5), i=0..5,
with exact zero at the endpoints. Amplitude is half the PUBLIC ToF zone width;
no private ray positions, target bearing or observed outcomes choose the scan.
Both arms receive six samples at.25s, elapsed1.25s, identical RGB640x360, original
8x8 ToF with up to two returns, four Radar slots and noisy IMU. Body/camera
positions, objects, textures, reflectance and initial RNG seeds match. Conditional
draws can diverge later. Entire co-located rig rotates, including Radar. Pitch
stays-3degrees, camera height1.7m. Motion blur is disabled in the inherited
renderer; physical head motion, comfort and hardware ToF response are untested.

This deliberately stationary condition isolates angular sampling without hidden
metric translation. It is not a walking deployment claim. Source/native yaw and
objects are evaluator-only. Predictors integrate public noisy delta_yaw from
initial forward alignment and never receive arm, family, source position or
target membership. Episode boundaries/times only reset causal state.

## Fixed predictors and useful measurements

Run unchanged MZ129 and frozen MZ143/MZ145 on both arms, with original model,
feature implementations and low/high thresholds. No fitting, cutoff selection,
source replacement, RGB-depth model or outcome-dependent scan changes. Scoring
the same predictors isolates observation effects; transfer from moving training
sources to stationary scans may fail and must not be called a sensing failure.
Seal both predictors' outputs before loading native evaluator labels.

Primary sensing question: does the scan recover native target contributions
within a causal six-frame budget? Report current and cumulative target-return
availability, first usable-return times, number of independent frames, strongest
slot versus all valid slots, geometric target hits before packet/detection
filtering, packets lost, per-case gains/losses, all families,
and inside/outside strata. Never equate missing samples with a clear corridor.
Returned points do not certify full surface coverage. Do not select a subset of
cases from their passive outcomes. Native per-return Radar ownership remains
outside the inherited evaluator contract; count public returns separately.

Report matched final-alert TP/FP/FN, precision/recall, events and first alerts,
