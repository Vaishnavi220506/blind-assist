## Main result

Across all240 frames, the fixed combination reduces nominal MZ108 FN35 to10,
with TP97 to122 and FP38 to41. All prior nominal and three-sensor positives
survive. The three additional FP are all on the separately declared 1cm-outside
stress object. This is a substantial controlled recall improvement, not an
unqualified nuisance reduction or natural-world breakthrough.

The **predeclared nonstress stratum** (216 frames,120 positive/96 negative)
improves nominal91TP/29FP/29FN to113TP/29FP/7FN. Recall75.83% becomes94.17%,
with identical false support and no positive lost. This is not a posthoc removal
of hard cases: both1cm stress episodes remain fully reported below and in the
overall result. Even in nonstress scenes,29FP remains a large unresolved burden.

## What causes the gain

The unchanged [support audit](mz111_support_audit.py) verifies all28 combined
decision changes (25 gainedTP/3 addedFP) against preceding/current measured
Radar, same-episode provenance and unique projected-box correspondence. None
has a missing source, age violation or ghost-to-object mismatch. All three new
FP are the genuinely outside stress object: correct conditional identity still
does not make the spatial proxy correct.

Temporal alone adds12TP/1FP, from12 distinct earlier measurements with ages
0.25/0.5s. It must keep current RGB correspondence and visible ambiguity; it
does not create a new measurement by incrementing an alert count. However,
the zero-velocity control gives108TP/39FP/24FN versus109/39/23 with Doppler.
The only Doppler benefit is one1cm-inside stress frame. Eleven of12 recovered
TP therefore survive with frozen range. The main demonstrated mechanism is
visual-correspondence-gated short range persistence, not a general motion model.

Simple0.5s alert hold gives117TP/51FP/15FN, compared with temporal109/39/23.
It buys eight extraTP for12 extraFP. Current visual/competition constraints
reduce that hold's false support, but neither method solves persistent ghosts.

## Decision and remaining work

Retain the no-ToF-gate association and current-RGB-gated short persistence as
`COMPONENT_OR_CHALLENGER / COMPONENT` within this controlled static-source scope.
Preserve the frozen combined challenger, all simpler controls, nominal and
interval baselines. Do not promote the plane/range filter as established extra
benefit, and do not tune margins/ages on these now-consumed scenes.

The active larger goal is **not complete**: we have improved missed-frame recall
and first-alert timing, but have not reduced nuisance against nominal at matched
recall/timing or established tracking through dynamic motion, occlusion, camera
pose errors or natural imagery. A further method needs decision-changing spatial
or temporal information; repeated hits and more elaborate geometry assumptions
alone are not established solutions. Both the substantial-object main result and
the failed boundary/ghost responsibilities stay visible in the next comparison.
