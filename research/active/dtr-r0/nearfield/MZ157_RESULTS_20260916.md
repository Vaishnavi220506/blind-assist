# MZ157: standalone relative depth does not recover metric thin geometry

EXPLORE consumed TRAIN capability experiment, 2026-09-16.
[Frozen protocol](MZ157_PROTOCOL_20260916.md).
Decision: `STOP_FIXED_RELATIVE_CALIBRATION_GEOMETRY_NOT_ADMITTED`.

**Both calibrations recover 0/423 rod columns. Regional modes improve shallow
boundary columns only from 60/955 to 73/955, while increasing erroneous near
depths around them.** Each arm produces a valid metric map on only 77/192 frames;
115 remain UNKNOWN. Three of five fixed geometry conditions fail. Stop this
recipe without tuning, new inference, dev/test scoring or alert integration.
Keep MZ129 and the scoped MZ145/MZ148/MZ156 components; App defaults are unchanged.

Post-outcome diagnostics qualify the cause: same-row comparisons retain some
rod and boundary relative ordering. These results reject this frozen global
metric calibration, not all RGB structure or every relative-depth method.

## Method and evidence authority

Use exactly the original consumed MZ136 TRAIN192, 48 frames per family, with one
RGB, original 8x8 two-return ToF, Radar and IMU. Extract and strict-load 239 frozen
DA2 Small keys (24,785,089 parameters) from the authenticated original DEPTHOR
checkpoint. Aspect-preserving 518 preprocessing produces a 518x924 network map;
resize relative inverse depth to original 360x640 before reciprocal/calibration.
No model download, weight training, new capture, dev/test or MZ146 decoding occurs.

Compare a regional median control with deterministic two-mode calibration of
the same prior. Both fit a positive affine inverse-depth mapping from public
ToF ranges, using arithmetic mean slant range of 32 sampled pixels as the region
proxy. Candidate assignment considers both two-return permutations and two fixed
starts, then chooses by public fit cost. Fit bounds, six assignment iterations,
Huber scale, validity checks and all thresholds were frozen before prediction.
At least six complete RGB-covered eligible zones are required; missing, merged
and partly outside-RGB zones do not calibrate the map. Their raw native evidence
is not deleted or vetoed. Inferred modes are hypotheses, not hit ownership.

This pixel-mean proxy does not reproduce reflectance-weighted native subray
aggregation. Median fits multiple valid slots to one mode; the candidate permits
distinct modes. Compared with historical full DEPTHOR, both resolution and
removal of learned fusion change. That historical contrast is not a causal
ablation. This implementation reproduces none of the complete literature methods
linked in the protocol.

All 192 predictions and calibration audits were sealed before native evaluator
decoding. References are visible collision AABBs, not dense rendered scene depth;
unrepresented surfaces remain UNKNOWN. The .12 m agreement tolerance is a geometry
diagnostic, not a new warning margin. A recovered column needs agreement on at
least half of its visible target pixels. Invalid predictions remain in coverage
denominators. No final alerts, event timing or independent native votes are scored.

## Saved-output diagnosis and limits

Exactly 100 frames have fewer than six eligible zones; a further 15 per arm
select zero slope. The attempted remaining solves converge without a small-
Jacobian rejection. Calibration validity by rod/boundary/BODY/HEAD is 33/3/37/4
for median and 32/4/37/4 for modes. Thus both insufficient usable anchors and
the selected global mapping limit this recipe.

An independent audit finds one selection limitation in
`mz136_near_rod_farwall_scene2_out_03`: the predefined lowest-cost selection picks
an invalid zero-slope fit although the other fixed start is valid. Selection
precedes validity. Preserve this behavior and failed result; no fallback/refit
is run after outcomes. It does not establish that a corrected selector would
restore useful geometry or pass the joint conditions.

Held-out-zone residual medians are .03469 m on 51 comparable frames and .02515 m
on 49. Held-out ranges still select mode correspondence, although they do not fit
the affine parameters. This is parameter-holdout consistency with different
denominators, not independent correspondence prediction or a clean accuracy gain.

Two posthoc diagnostics use saved relative maps only. An initial pooled
target-versus-far-ring ordering measure is confounded by a strong vertical prior
trend seen in first-frame panels. Preserve it and add a same-image-row comparison
without fitting or changing predictions. For each frame, AUC measures the fraction
of target/far-ring pairs ranked in the correct inverse-depth order (ties count
half); the table averages frames, and each family has 48 comparable frames.

Local rod/boundary ordering is partly present; the initial pooled statistic
cannot justify saying all thin RGB geometry disappeared. Same-row ordering is
still evaluator-conditioned, posthoc and neither a metric distance estimate nor
an alert gain. Suspended HEAD remains weak under this control. Any future route
must distinguish local ordering, metric identification and final warning quality;
these diagnostics do not authorize tuning or promote a successor recipe.
