# Fixed-geometry appearance intervention

EXPLORE, user-authorized 2026-09-21. One 1152-frame capture and frozen B/N inference;
no fit, cutoff change, protected test or default change. Question: do target or
background appearance interventions change current B/N decisions with identical
geometry and public ToF, beyond an identical-scene re-render control?

Select the lexicographically first TRAIN group of each of the four families in
the completed data-coverage source spec, before reading their predictions. Reuse
all three relations and all 24 frames. These four groups are consumed training
data for N, not independent validation. For each clip capture reference, exact
repeat, target-material-only, background-material-only, in that order. Target
alternative is Sage unless already Sage, then Cream; background alternative is
Brick unless already Brick, then Wood. All other scene fields remain identical.

Capture uses the existing map, primitive geometry, 640x360 camera, renderer
readiness, 32/16 warmup schedule and native depth extraction. One UE allocation,
1800 seconds including startup; mechanical errors preserve their receipts and
may be repaired without changing cases, materials or scientific rules. No scene
replacement or outcome-based recapture. Release only task-owned processes.

Before inference, require equal geometry/camera, exact native-depth arrays and
exact simulated public ranges (including missing states) for every paired frame.
If this source condition fails, report appearance-only inference NOT_EVALUABLE;
do not attribute any model change exclusively to appearance. Save all differences.
The original depth-only single-return law and group/time noise identity are kept;
this deliberately does not simulate material reflectance effects on real ToF.

Use old B weights and its saved B_control cutoff 6.888704776763916; N uses its
saved cutoff 7.03014612197876. Encoder and model bytes are hashed before capture.
Report logits, supplement bits, original A OR supplement and unchanged 0.2s
nonrecursive hold. Preserve A/UNKNOWN. Seal observations and predictions before
joining evaluator truth; public inference receives only RGB and public ranges.

Primary evidence is paired decision-flip rate and absolute-logit change, by
model, condition, family, relation and complete clip. Re-render flip rate is the
nuisance control. Call a condition decision-sensitive only when it exceeds the
repeat control in at least two of the four groups and at least 1% of all 288
paired frames (3 frames); report all raw differences regardless. Failure of this
criterion means NOT_SUPPORTED_FOR_THIS_INTERVENTION, not all appearance invariance.
Logit-only change is a separate diagnostic, not an alert gain.

Report current/held TP/FP/FN, precision/recall/FPR, UNKNOWN, false segments,
event detection and first in-event alert delay. Keep Core/Boundary separate and
all pre-entry/post-exit negatives. No promotion question is answered here.
Positive: retain the mechanism evidence and propose data/representation work;
negative: deprioritize this specific explanation; invalid inputs: not evaluable.
All outcomes end this pilot without a new training arm or repeated material search.
