## Mechanism and scope

[Joint association](mz114_joint_association.py) enumerates all injective current
assignments including unmatched, retains global alternatives within best+0.5,
and resolves only unanimous nonnull pairs. Temporal costs use actual LK image
correspondence and earlier measured range, with finite Doppler or explicit
unknown-velocity hold. Only a new resolved current measurement refreshes range
age; neither an image match nor repeated alerts manufacture a new measurement.

Missing prior adds no contradiction. Invalid IMU blocks new association/point
support from cached yaw and falls back to supplied baseline/independent ToF.
The pre-freeze IMU fallback defect was corrected and tested before any candidate
outcomes. This is not an after-result parameter or rule adjustment.

Compatibility costs and the plane readout remain assumptions, not calibrated
identity probabilities or measured per-pixel depth. Both conceptual references
in the protocol are primary research sources; neither DELTAR nor CR3DT models or
published benchmark scores are reproduced by this implementation.

## Decision and verification

Record the fixed joint current/temporal alert replacement as intended
`NEGATIVE_CONTROL`. Keep the established MZ113 challenger and the failed fixed
candidate for related falsification; do not tune the consumed margin/range cost.
The hypothesis that this global-assignment/range prior produces a matched
nuisance improvement is rejected on this scope. No fresh expansion is justified.
The multiple-hypothesis evidence schema remains available for diagnosis, not an
alert promotion. The larger four-sensor optimization goal remains active.

The next information study should address finite ToF footprints, multiple depth
components and their relationship to local RGB boundaries. It must distinguish
documented output fields from hypothetical signal/detection assumptions and
include thin structures that still fail detection. It cannot replace a zone
measurement with hidden exact subray or native actor identity. This study has
not yet implemented or validated that forward model. The primary-source notes
in `zonal-model-design.md` distinguish ST's reported-range noise estimate from
the hidden spatial depth variance inside a zone, and strongest-first ordering
from nearest-first. These are different quantities; do not conflate them when
building a hypothetical sensor model. See [ST UM2884, sections4.9--5.5](https://www.st.com/resource/en/user_manual/um2884-a-guide-to-using-the-vl53l5cx-multizone-timeofflight-ranging-sensor-with-a-wide-field-of-view-ultra-lite-driver-uld-stmicroelectronics.pdf)
and the [DELTAR sensing explanation](https://zju3dv.github.io/deltar/).
