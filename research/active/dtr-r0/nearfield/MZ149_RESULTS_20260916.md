## Frozen method and inputs

MZ149 reuses the saved scores of MZ148's compensated HGB without retraining
vision features or model weights. A two-state forward filter uses transition,
initial-state and class-prior frequencies from original TRAIN192, with one
fixed Laplace count per category. Classifier odds divided by TRAIN prior odds
serve as an emission proxy. Each episode or non-.25s gap resets the state;
there is no backward pass, future input or evaluator geometry in inference.
Correlated classifier evidence does not yield calibrated probabilities merely
by passing through this filter. State zero and no-alert remain UNKNOWN.

For each whole-scene OOF fold, transition parameters are counted from the other
three folds and used to filter that fold's saved scores. The threshold is the
minimum filtered OOF score on incumbent TRAIN true frames:
`.012839115828887094`. Full-TRAIN transition counts are `[[76,4],[4,76]]`,
giving stay probability `.9390243902439024` and change probability
`.06097560975609756`. Both initial-positive and class priors are `.5`.
Each fold's training counts are `[[57,3],[3,57]]`. OOF TRAIN gives96/43/0
TP/FP/FN. These parameters and the threshold are sealed before Development
scores are read; no additional parameter search was performed.
