EXPLORE, 2026-09-16. A separately scoped structural follow-up to MZ143's partial
alert gain and extra nuisance segments. No MZ143 fitted model or threshold is
changed. No MZ137 or MZ139 frozen result is rerun or rewritten.

## Mechanism

Replace generic learned alarm scores with a signed geometric intrusion margin.
Use all public VALID return slots and all observed RGB proposals, including
the existing narrow-edge refinement. Project each return's slant range and
uncertainty through the part of its zone overlapping the visual proposal.
Find a body-forward depth mode supported by at least two distinct zones;
slots within a zone are alternatives, not independent corroboration.
Refine the depth estimate using public ROI ray directions and the regional
mean-range equation for a constant-reflectance front plane. Retain competing
modes and range-to-region uncertainty in the audit. No native hit, identity,
depth map, truth mask or evaluator geometry enters prediction.

The plane and source assignment remain hypotheses. The scalar is the minimum
signed overlap with the declared forward/lateral/height corridor, maximized
over eligible visual surface hypotheses. Missing geometry returns UNKNOWN with
a -10 sentinel. It never means clearance.

Independently retain the frozen MZ129 Radar branch, raw ToF enclosures entirely
inside the corridor, and possible raw ToF support outside/partly outside RGB
using the original column weights. No fine RGB boundary can veto these branches.
Within jointly observed space the new direct margin replaces diffuse unmatched
possible-support votes; this is a changed decision mechanism, unlike MZ137's
per-return replacement while every residual vote remained alarm-owning.
