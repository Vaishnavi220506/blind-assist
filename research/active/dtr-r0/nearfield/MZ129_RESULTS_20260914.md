139 TP / 93 FP / 5 FN, versus MZ128's 139 / 103 / 5.** All 139 incumbent true
frames and all 15 events retain their original first-alert times. False segments
fall from 26 to 22. ToF interval subdivision adds no frame-level benefit, alone
or in combination. Retain the simpler Radar correction as a scoped Development
component; MZ116 and the application default remain unchanged.

## What changed and what stayed independent

[Radar code](mz129_radar_box_correction.py) gives the frozen MZ111 current plane
and range-filter branch MZ125's corrected observable boxes. Those boxes were
previously used for ToF allocation while Radar retained the original boxes.
The update can change association and temporal range matching as well as extent;
this is a corrected-geometry package, not a box-only causal contrast.
Six of the ten removed false frames use exactly the same range before/after,
isolating the changed spatial box. Four also have changed filtered ranges
because the boxes alter temporal association; no separate frozen-range arm
was run, so their contribution is attributed to the package.

The candidate independently ORs current raw center-in-corridor Radar, corrected
current geometry, all original-box flow carry, and original/new resolution guards.
Original common-Radar bits and original guards reproduce on all 288 frames.
Replaying the original MZ113 flow recovers 17 propagated records, with 11 frames
of carry support and one overlap hidden by the old current-Radar OR. The two
previously diagnosed Radar-only carry FP remain alerting. No carry is inferred
solely from `old_common AND NOT current` or dropped to improve scores.

The original OpenCV environment is not identical to this replay environment.
The saved 288 output unions match, but historical internal flow values are not
claimed bit-identical. The complete recomputed original-box carry is preserved
with the frozen .5 s age limit and unchanged correspondence rules.

Explicit raw protection adds three branch supports relative to the original
common/guard union; corrected-box guards occur on four frames. Their influence
is retained even when a corrected RGB box would reject the return. This does
not reproduce MZ124's different broad ±12-degree raw-null uncertainty policy,
nor certify that missing RGB or range evidence means free space.

Current associated-Radar support is withdrawn on 15 frames: ten false frames
and five true frames. All five true frames retain raw support, and four also
retain ToF. `mz123_substantial_body_pair0_in_06` has ToF score zero and remains
alerting through raw support plus its new resolution guard. Independent
protection is therefore material to retention, not just bookkeeping. The three
raw additions relative to the old branch all already had ToF alerts, so those
three additions alone change no whole-frame output. The post-seal independent
audit is saved in `radar-final-attribution.json` under the evidence root.

## Why the ToF refinement is not retained in the candidate

[Readout code](mz129_extent_correction.py) conserves all 3,954 returned records,
the saved ROIs, range bounds and status, and the MZ128 weights/protections. The
existing depth-six subdivision proves 56 previously intersecting return
enclosures disjoint. All 56 have positive binary weight and SIM_VALID status;
55 are whole-zone unresolved and only one uses a localized association ROI.
None contains a native corridor-hit sample. Three samples belong to a hazardous
actor elsewhere, which is distinct from a sample inside the corridor; no
incumbent true frame is lost.

Other supports still sustain all affected alerts, so no frame changes. Every
MERGED return retains the complete .02–4 m range; none is excluded by this
refinement. This result is consistent with MZ124's limited whole-zone benefit
and does not justify adding refinement cost to the retained Radar candidate.
