## What the follow-up ruled out

The completed contrast does not support finer RGB resampling as a sufficient
repair of MZ153 under this frozen model/input interface. Missing current rod
returns remain0/175 recovered columns in all three arms. The resolution change
did not extract the desired unmeasured rod surface. This is not a proof that
RGB lacks useful information or that all learned temporal reconstruction fails.

A separate post-outcome source-opportunity diagnostic checks only48 TRAIN rod
frames. Of the24 current-return-absent frames, six have an earlier selected rod
return within their existing causal prefix and18 have none. Across the eight
six-frame episodes, camera displacement from the first frame is only2.42–3.61cm
and yaw span0.288–0.538degrees, versus the public5.625degree ToF cell width.
These are native source diagnostics, not predictor pose inputs or proof of a
new active-sensing gain. They distinguish carrying earlier evidence from needing
a more informative observation. No motion controller, new trajectory or capture
has been evaluated here.

The rod target's world Y follows camera/body-origin Y to within1e-12m in all
eight episodes, while its world X/Z stay fixed. Other saved background objects
remain world-static. Thus lateral camera translation is canceled by target
co-motion in these rod tracks; forward approach remains. This is a scoped source
property, not a claim that the source is defective. These sequences cannot alone
adjudicate reconstruction from lateral views around a world-static obstacle.
That is a distinct observation-design question, with changed motion/input
conditions and their costs requiring an explicit comparison before claiming gain.
