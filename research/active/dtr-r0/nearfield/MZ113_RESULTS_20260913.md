## Decision, verification and delivery

Retain optical correspondence as `COMPONENT_OR_CHALLENGER / COMPONENT` for
short measured-range continuity through moving visual regions. Preserve the
incumbent and the same-policy angular ablation. The fixed nuisance-improvement
hypothesis is not supported: no post-exit benefit and one extra ordinary-crossing
FP. This is a controlled tracking contribution with a spatial error tradeoff,
not an unconditional mainline alert replacement or a nuisance breakthrough.

The broader optimization goal remains active. Keep independent sensor support,
HEIGHT_UNKNOWN and UNKNOWN semantics. The next decision needs improved spatial
evidence/ambiguity or observably distinct ghost evidence; more alert repetition
or tuning these now-consumed margins does not supply it. Zonal-footprint ToF,
reference-direction uncertainty and a full matched miss/nuisance curve remain
separate untested responsibilities.
