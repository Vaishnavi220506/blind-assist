## Limited discrimination gain and cutoff-support diagnostic

The saved OOF comparison on the **same original192 frames** improves AUC from
`.9187825521` to `.9479166667` and log loss from `.4066394677` to
`.3161834824`. At the diagnostic score.5, TP/FP/FN changes81/16/15 to84/15/12.
This is limited evidence that adding training configurations improves score
discrimination on those matched held-out folds. It is neither fresh validation
nor evidence of improved final alerts. Training sets differ by design, and this
diagnostic did not select a new cutoff or model. Across TRAIN480, the actual
OOF causal operating point gives240/133/0; single-frame low-cutoff gives240/150/0.

The fixed extreme-support calibration is constrained by two low-scoring
positive training observations:

- `mz146_shallow_boundary_stress_scene3_exit_02` sets low to
  `.00829862256322582`.
- First frame `mz146_suspended_head_scene3_in_00` sets high to
  `.032424448336891454`, with no preceding score available.

Original MZ145 low/high were `.020014435971556582` and
`.2957935335969224`. The rare boundary/head support therefore forces lower
cutoffs under the unchanged selection rule, even as average discrimination
improves. The resulting final alerts remain inadequate. This identifies the
exact numerical cutoff support and a plausible failure mechanism; it does not
uniquely establish causality or exclude changes elsewhere in the score
distribution. No cutoff rescue, fold change or source-membership change was
performed after scoring.

The expanded model weights are retained as an artifact that may be reused in
a separately justified new structure. The unchanged complete recipe is not an
alert challenger and its failed consumed run must not be relabelled successful.
