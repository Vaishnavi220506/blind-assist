## What was actually tested

The new function receives ToF packet availability and raw zones only. It links
four-neighbor zones with exactly one SIM_VALID target using the inherited strict
slant-depth gap <.30 m. Each cohort requires at least two distinct zones and
global max-minus-min depth <.30 m; multi-target, MERGED, malformed, single-zone
and depth-conflict cases retain their existing footprint. Of 246 connected
cohorts, 208 qualify and 38 fail global depth span. Across all 3,954 returns,
1,245 footprints narrow. The 266 non-valid/MERGED, 70 multi-target-zone returns
and 738 depth-conflict returns remain unchanged.

For each axis the candidate uses the equal-zone angular centroid and maximum
participating center deviation as half-width. Axes with no center variation
retain full native bounds. Intersect this envelope with the existing MZ129 ROI;
empty intersections fall back. Keep each return's range and pose uncertainty.
No new RGB mask, box, association, signal weighting or image-boundary clipping
enters the cohort construction. Existing MZ129 RGB allocation remains inherited.

This deliberately operationalizes **center-position span**, not the full span
of physical zone boundaries. Their full-bound union cannot narrow any member
zone. The center-span formula therefore adds an uncalibrated edge-exclusion
assumption beyond depth connectivity. It was disclosed before scoring; the
result must not be described as testing every possible interpretation of the
user's ToF-native angular evidence proposal.

All 7,182 contributing samples in narrowed returns have evaluable lineage and
projection. Of previously contained samples, 2,371 fall outside the new
footprint, including 467 on corridor-intersecting actors. **158 samples are
themselves inside the corridor**, spanning 54 returns in 24 frames. These are
sample/frame counts, not 158 obstacles or 158 new FN.

A direct counterexample is `mz123_suspended_head_pair1_in_00`, zone 19, slot 0,

possible and the frame still alerts. This is native angular edge loss, not the

The lost rod TP is a separate effect: zone 44's last positive-weight possible

## Decision, validation and reproduction

observes it, but cannot define either sensor's complete coverage. MZ131 adds a
second constraint: same-depth adjacent positive zones justify a cohort, but
their centroid alone is not a demonstrated bound on all contributing surfaces.
Keep the useful support-reach diagnosis; reject the exact center-span rule.
No post-score edge pad, fallback tuning, MZ130 repair or successor was run.
