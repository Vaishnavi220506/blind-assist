## What is learned, and what is already known

The prior-only replacement imposed an unnecessary prerequisite on the point
proxy: a supported proxy IN disappeared whenever the finite bank had no matching
shape. Treating shape as supplemental positive evidence removes that prerequisite
on eight cases and allows six further shape-supported positives. Preservation
comes from the OR algebra. It does not show the prior independently learned to
retain measurements or solve out-of-distribution geometry.

This is **a toy responsibility analogy to an established project mechanism**.
[Camera-forward complementarity](SPATIAL_COMPLEMENT_RESULTS_20260921.md) already
uses A OR B and explicitly warns that retention does not control newly added FP.
[MZ175](MZ175_RESULTS_20260916.md) also ORs an ambiguous-Radar task-consensus branch
with the complete incumbent and therefore cannot reduce incumbent FP. The new
work changes the role of this particular toy shape candidate; it is not novelty
evidence and does not reopen or supersede either historical test.

The weakest assumption is that the shape-only IN additions stay trustworthy
when the true object and image formation do not belong to the ideal prior.
All six additions here are closed-world cases with perfect sampled silhouette,
known noiseless calibration and a true shape already in the bank. That is a
favorable mechanism fixture. OR inevitably preserves point false positives and
admits shape false positives if they occur; a focused test verifies that such an
addition is counted as FP instead of hidden. No heldout source tests this risk.
The public zonal centre/range proxy is not a physically identified returned point
or independently certified obstacle support. None of the64ranges has been
relocalized or upgraded in authority by this comparison.

The brief was fixed before the new readout pass, but its useful overlap was known
from consumed results. Therefore48TP is a disclosed posthoc toy Development
number, not a fresh confirmation, current Calibration gain, proof of hardware
adequacy, general RGB benefit or user safety. Static cases do not establish event
recall, onset delay, false-alert durations or motion costs.
