## One new causal rule

low cutoff0.020014435971556582 stay unchanged. High onset cutoff
0.2957935335969224 is determined only from its OOF TRAIN scores and incumbent
true frames requiring immediate onset. No dev score is loaded until that value
and code are sealed. These cutoffs are empirical score thresholds, not calibrated
probabilities.

Current score above the high cutoff alerts immediately. Otherwise both current
and immediately preceding scores must reach the low cutoff. Episode boundaries
and timestamp gaps reset prior evidence. No future input, backdating, additional
model fitting, per-family threshold or full-incumbent OR is used. Strong true
onsets in this dev panel alert immediately; weak isolated false spikes are removed.
The rule can delay weak first evidence in other scenes, which fresh validation
must measure rather than assume away.
