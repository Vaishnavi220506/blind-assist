## Structural change

The fixed observation-only extractor retains 128 ToF slots and their angular
footprints, distance, sigma, signal, status and body-frame interval support;
four Radar slots; and causal IMU orientation. It adds native-resolution RGB
proposal/range hypotheses with signed corridor overlap, foreground quantiles,
and boundary-gradient measurements. It has 2,217 sensor and 268 geometry
features, no learned frontend, no labels, family, scene, pair identity or
incumbent alarm input. Merged returns remain broad regional evidence rather
than exact pixel depths. RGB cannot delete a slot; every original packet stays
available for audit. Input retention is not a guarantee of a retained alarm.

This differs from MZ136's learned pooled image-cell representation and MZ140's
dense-depth regression. All three ExtraTrees candidates use identical fixed
settings; the second fused readout uses fixed histogram boosting. Four complete
scene-group folds produce OOF TRAIN scores, thresholds and model selection.
Models refit on TRAIN192 and seal before any dev prediction or dev truth parse.
There is no dev threshold search or post-result model selection.
