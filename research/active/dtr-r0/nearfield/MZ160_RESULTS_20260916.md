## Coverage and cause of failure

There are 60 warmup frames and 180 frames with two causal image pairs, for 360
pairs total. Saved public products contain 40,989,453 valid-disparity pixel
occurrences across pairs. These repeat pixels and times; they are neither
independent observations nor verified metric depths.

| Fixed calibration stage | Pairs |
| --- | ---: |
| Fewer than 8 eligible ToF anchor zones | 152 |
| At least 8 zones, inverse-depth span below 0.03/m | 192 |
| Initial count/span sufficient, later fixed fit checks fail | 15 |
| At least one query-excluded model accepted | 1 |

The single accepted pair belongs to the moving-crossing-rod episode. No frame
has accepted models at both lags; no static-lateral frame obtains a model.
Thus added actor/rod points, correctly ranged rod frames and covered actor
episodes are all zero. The saved-product diagnosis does not rerun matching or
calibration and makes no outcome-driven parameter changes.

This locates the failure before final point fusion: independent range anchors
are absent or too concentrated in depth for this free slope/intercept fit.
More matched pixels alone do not resolve that scale problem. It does not prove
a fundamental fixed-hardware limit, nor reject every temporal or motion method.
Depth containment is **not estimable with zero accepted points**; the scorer's
numeric zero is only a failed-gate convention. Zero control/context errors are
vacuous and do not demonstrate geometric accuracy.

tests pass. Before real matching, the recipe freezes 42 local source files and
the exact capture inputs. Public geometry, disparity maps, RGB hashes and both

uses OpenCV 4.10.0 in 32.474 s, and evaluation takes 1.502 s. These are desktop

there is no CUDA or Android latency claim. One initial geometry process fails
before creating outputs because tools/research_backend.py is absent from its
module path. A task-process PYTHONPATH entry fixes the launch environment;
frozen code, parameters and inputs remain unchanged. No inference restarts
after real matching, fitting or outcomes. All task-owned processes have exited;
there is no worker, listener or paid allocation.

Reproduction uses run_mz160_dense_parallax.py modes freeze, baseline, geometry,
