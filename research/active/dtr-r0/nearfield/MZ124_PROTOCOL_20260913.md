## Question, controls and limits

Can diagnosis, measurement-centered geometry, accountable alert correction,
paired training, or causal temporal evidence improve the recall/nuisance tradeoff
without discarding independently credible near-obstacle support?

Use all 288 consumed MZ123 frames and all 24 complete episodes. Keep the original
MZ116 and frozen early-at-0.58 predictions. Native geometry, source roles and
labels are evaluator/training-only; predictors receive observable packets/RGB
or their sealed observable-derived predictions. Missing or ambiguous support is
UNKNOWN, not clearance. This is controlled synthetic Development only.

1. **Diagnosis:** compare true-core, wrong-core and off-core frozen scores in
   missed HEAD, rod/wall and BODY pairs; inspect observable support and paired
   output changes. Threshold feasibility is posthoc diagnosis, not rescue.
2. **Measurement-centered geometry:** use a finite ToF return's complete angular
   footprint and range bounds, preserving merged/ambiguous returns. Compare
   enclosing Cartesian boxes with bounded interval subdivision of the original
   ray/range support. Keep all RGB association alternatives plus a null
   explanation when association completeness is not established. Report the
   three-sensor versus RGB-assisted arm separately, without claiming echo identity.
3. **Accountable correction:** remove an incumbent alert only when its eligible
   current ToF support is proved disjoint by the bounded geometry calculation,
   with no incumbent Radar/guard support, merged-return ambiguity, invalid IMU
   or missing packet used as clearance. Report removed TP/FP and retained unknown
   cases. The rule is a tested geometric proxy, not a safety certificate.
4. **Paired training:** one matched BCE versus BCE plus changed-cell ranking
   contrast, weight 0.25, margin 1; identical OccupancyNet initialization,
   800 steps, batch eight (four whole pairs), AdamW 0.001/0.0001, paired
   synchronous brightness/contrast augmentation. Pair0/1 train (192 frames),
   pair2 diagnostic development (96 frames). This partition is untrained for
   these fits but already consumed and related, never fresh validation. One
   seed and at most 20 minutes per fit; no outcome-driven retries or extra fit.
   Preserve fixed 0.58 comparisons plus original joint-selector Development
   curves; absent feasibility stays absent, no substituted selected point.
5. **Time:** fixed causal one-frame hold and two-of-last-three replay with
   startup/episode reset rules; also inspect whether short histories contain
   correct evidence. Do not infer metric translation from IMU rotation. Any
   spatial time variant requires a defensible coordinate/motion assumption.

Accept useful Development components only when the comparison explains their
gain. Report frame TP/FP/FN, precision/recall, HEAD retention, complete-event
retention, detected-event delay with misses explicit, false-frame duration and
false segments together. No false segments become real notifications/minute.
Use paired answers and spatial metrics where the arm actually predicts them.
Do not fabricate occupancy for MZ116 or a binary postprocessor.

Each direction ends after its stated contrast and focused checks, irrespective
of gain. A negative or inconclusive result does not launch more optimization.
Small metadata/interval work uses CPU as TASK_NOT_GPU_SUITABLE; learning uses
the registered worker GPU with actual backend evidence. Preserve input hashes,
predictions, models and diagnostics under `artifacts.local/work/`; release owned
processes/jobs after completion. Register via the knowledge CLI only; preserve
