# MZ111: competing spatial readouts and causal range continuation

EXPLORE, 2026-09-13. Active user goal authorizes multiple optimization methods;
this bounded comparison does not redefine the goal as a successful small test.
MZ110 finds 20 range-absent, six ToF-gated and four geometry-limited nominal FN.
Test three spatial contrasts and one causal continuation, then select a useful
unchanged method for a separately authored new 240-frame Development panel.

Inputs are the authenticated 288 consumed MZ107--109 frames and cached actual
RGB proposals/IMU integrations. Baseline, MZ108 nominal and MZ109 interval remain.
No threshold sweep, scene-specific parameters, true pose/range/identity inputs,
sensor-model replacement or larger RGB network. All inference precedes evaluator
parsing; source geometry/identities score outcomes only.

Spatial A: keep every angle-compatible RGB/Radar explanation (existing 12 degree
tolerance); use reciprocal unique pairs to refine. ToF is optional corroboration,
not a required gate. Existing nominal radial-shell geometry is the first arm.
Spatial B: replace the radial shell by a camera-frontoparallel plane through the
measured range at the region center. This is an explicit surface approximation,
not inferred surface normal or exact dense depth. Same pairs and fallback.
Spatial C: same plane with a causal scalar range filter on unique visual tracks.
Compensate image angular boxes by measured yaw; IoU >=0.2 and mutual uniqueness
are required for continuity. Range prior uses last observed Doppler and dt,
maximum age 0.5s, measurement sigma 0.08m and process sigma 0.15m/s. An innovation
larger than 0.35m resets rather than smoothing different objects. Do not lower
uncertainty or create confidence from repeated alert counts. No range continuation
without a present Radar measurement in this spatial contrast.

Temporal D is an additive contrast on original nominal: only a current RGB
region with unique causal correspondence to a past measured range can add
support during a Radar gap. Propagate range by measured Doppler; age <=0.5s.
No future image, range or truth access. Current ambiguous measurements do not
become a gap that permits old evidence. Invalid IMU, timestamps, missing visual
continuity and ambiguity reset relevant tracks. No ghost suppression claim.

Also report one predeclared combination: spatial C OR additions unique to
temporal D (D AND NOT nominal). This does not automatically restore nominal
positives that spatial C removed. Keep individual contrasts interpretable.

Each support preserves sensor origin, evidence age, range/box and ambiguity;
unassociated Radar retains HEIGHT_UNKNOWN, no support stays UNKNOWN. Preserve
independent ToF. Initial body reference is not future walking intent.

Report all arms, TP/FP/FN/UNKNOWN by panel/family, baseline and nominal TP lost,
positive event segments with no alert, first alert delay measured only while
truth-positive, false alert segment counts/duration, and implementation cost.
Sampling is discrete .25s; duration uses one .25s bin per frame, not user-route
or physical timing. Add a current-RGB-disabled control for candidate dependence.

Retain a candidate for fresh evaluation only if there is a decision-relevant
tradeoff, including absolute HEAD/pole recall and no hidden denominator change.
Do not call a small controlled gain a breakthrough. Stop this fixed comparison,
preserve negatives, then continue the authorized larger goal with justified
method/source work. Registration failures in the unrelated old ledger remain
reported evidence gaps, not permission to fabricate a registry entry.
