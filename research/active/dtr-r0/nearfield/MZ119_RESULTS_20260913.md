## Geometry and failure explanation

There are 144 accepted pose pairs on 94 frames: 70 permit zero translation and
74 exclude it. Only four frames produce depth points, all in the moving-barrier
episode (`mz119_moving_barrier_head_08` through `_11`). Of their 55 points,
42 intervals contain the native actor AABB first-hit reference. The other 13
place context at approximately **1.44-3.09 m** when its first AABB hit is
**12.22-13.13 m**: ten background points and three floor points.

Seventeen pose bounds across ten frames exclude the commanded-reference
translation. All use actually moving barrier anchors; nine pairs have only
moving anchors. A coherent moving foreground can supply a strong correspondence
consensus while violating the static-anchor assumption and misplacing background.
More consensus alone therefore does not establish camera motion.

Important authority correction to the frozen protocol/audit field names:
`evaluator.camera` equals the commanded frozen source pose, not an independent
native camera-actor readback. Translation containment is a **commanded-reference
pose check**, even where the audit JSON key says `native_translation`. Actor
bounds are read from the engine; ray first hits use those AABBs plus frozen
background/floor boxes. They are analytic geometry references, not native depth
images or pixel segmentation, and omit unmodeled rendering detail.

The 38 accepted rotation/stationary/low-motion control pairs all admit zero
translation and produce zero points. No query reuses its own ToF pose anchor;
no audited reference/current actor identity mismatch occurs. Coverage is sparse:
146 frames have no accepted pose pair, 42 have no nonzero pair, 30 have one and
22 have two; 18 of those 22 still produce no points. Stored metadata cannot
separate feature exclusion from query-bound rejection in those 18 frames.
No additional replay was performed to subdivide that diagnostic.
