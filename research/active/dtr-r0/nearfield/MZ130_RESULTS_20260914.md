# MZ130: local masks give no alert gain and expose a field-of-view exclusion

**All four arms remain 139 TP / 93 FP / 5 FN. Keep MZ129.** The fixed local
support candidate does not reduce a single false frame or false segment. Its
ToF mask restriction also excludes one previously retained native corridor
point outside the RGB image, despite the frame staying alert. Reject this exact
mask-as-complete-support rule; retained frame recall alone concealed its defect.

Terminal: `MZ130_LOCAL_SUPPORT_NO_GAIN_RGB_FOV_EXCLUSION`; intended inheritance
`NEGATIVE_CONTROL` for this fixed image-clipped local-support recipe. The exact
mask encoder remains useful implementation evidence, not occupancy validation.
The [pre-score brief](MZ130_LOCAL_SUPPORT_BRIEF_20260914.md) fixes construction,
four arms and native-retention acceptance before the new outcomes are scored.

## Fixed observable mechanism

[Mask materializer](mz130_local_masks.py) recomputes the existing MZ125 foreground
from authenticated RGB. All 288 box outputs reproduce. Each component is
independently dilated by the existing two-pixel pad and clipped to its padded
box/image. Exact run rectangles preserve holes and disconnected row support;
no convex hull, center estimate or uniform box shrink is substituted. The pack
contains 643 valid components and 4,017 rectangles; 24 frames retain old-box
fallback and 14 have no proposal. Pixel-by-pixel reconstruction checks pass.

[Local-support code](mz130_local_support.py) uses positive mask-area/signal
agreement across at least two distinct ToF zones with the unchanged depth,
coverage, cosine and margin rules. Accepted masks must not conflict with an
existing association. A return uses the union of clipped local tiles with its
unchanged range and pose bounds. MERGED retains its whole original ROI and
.02–4 m bounds. Missing, ambiguous or empty matches retain old support.

Radar uses unchanged MZ129 associations, filtered distances and plane anchor.
It localizes only when at least two ToF zones support the same component and
their horizontal-range intervals agree with current raw Radar within the fixed
working bound. Its tiles are their local union within the inherited angular
compatibility window. Raw Radar, complete original carry and all guards remain
independent. The Radar-only arm therefore tests cross-sensor correspondence
without altering the independent ToF alert bit; it is not a Radar-only input.

Of 260 valid ToF cohorts, 130 pass the local signal test, 121 remain unresolved
and nine have insufficient/competing zones. The recipe localizes 815 returned
targets but creates **zero new ToF associations**. Its evidence does not resolve
the previously unassociated whole-zone returns. Radar localizes 103 of 348
current returns; 176 lack a unique existing association and 69 lack two
range-compatible local ToF zones. All fallbacks keep their original support.

In the remaining 93 false frames, 422 positive-weight ToF return supports remain:
152 valid returns are localized, 188 valid returns are not localized, and 82
MERGED returns stay unchanged. Only 32 false frames have localized triggering
support, all boundary cases; 61 false frames have no triggering return touched.
Twelve BODY false frames contain some localized returns, but those are not the
returns sustaining the alert. The 25 rod and three HEAD false frames have no
ToF localization. No individual ToF possible-support bit changes.

Radar withdraws three current support bits, all in true boundary frames:
`mz123_shallow_boundary_stress_pair2_enter_11`, `pair2_exit_01`, `pair2_exit_02`
(the latter two share the same `mz123_shallow_boundary_stress_` prefix).
Their raw/carry/guard branches are false; independent ToF scores 4/3/4 alone
preserve the alerts. These withdrawals are not a component-level nuisance gain.

## Native retention falsifies the complete-mask assumption

The post-seal audit examines 4,186 contributing native ToF samples in the 815
localized returns. Sixty-seven previously retained samples fall outside the
new tile union; all 67 project outside the RGB image. Forty-one belong to actors
that intersect the corridor, and one sample itself is inside the corridor.
These are different counts and do not mean 41 newly missed obstacles.

The decisive point is in `mz123_shallow_boundary_stress_pair0_exit_00`, ToF zone
61, slot 0, native sample 6. Its world point is approximately
(2.45125, .27000, .59658) m, and its image projection is (372.574, 360.596).
Image height is 360 pixels. The original ROI extends to y=362, within the ToF
zone extending to about y=369.3; the new mask tiles end at y=360. Thus an RGB
field-of-view limit erases an independently observed ToF location. The return
still has possible support and the frame remains alert, so aggregate TP/FN
alone does not reveal the evidence loss.

This is not an in-image segmentation miss: all newly removed ToF contributors
are outside the image. The fixed recipe implemented its declared image clip,
but that recipe violates the intended no-missing-modality-veto principle at
the coverage boundary. It is an algorithmic failure, not an acceptable mask
refinement or evidence that the obstacle disappeared.

For Radar, 103 localized real-return native hit points are evaluable. Two points
formerly inside their associated boxes are outside the new unions; neither
point is in the corridor, while one belongs to a corridor-intersecting actor.
The native hit is reconstructed on the camera-to-actor-center trace using the
authenticated pre-noise horizontal range, consistent with the simulator's
source implementation. This is simulated lineage, not RF surface recovery.

There is no missing native projection in either audited set. Even zero sampled
hazard losses would not prove full continuous-surface coverage. No evaluator
pixel, actor ID or point selects a component, tile, association or output.

## Decision and reproducibility

Do not add this exact local restriction to MZ129. Carry two failure signatures:
positive contour matching did not resolve new associations or reduce nuisance,
and image coverage cannot define the entire possible support of a ToF return.
A future separately scoped change must explicitly preserve the unobserved
portion of an echo footprint; that change is not tuned or run in this experiment.
