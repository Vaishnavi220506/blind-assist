## Geometry and measurement admission

Every zone was regenerated from source scene cubes, floor and background using
finite first-hit ray/AABB intersections. Neither interpolated 8x8 measurements
nor native hit coordinates enter the predictor. Before scoring, legacy8 analytic
hits were compared with all165888 saved native UE rays: hit/miss agreement is
exact, all32435 hit ranges agree within the predeclared1e-5m tolerance, maximum
error6.94e-7m. Returned count, status and quantized range reproduce exactly;
maximum absolute signal discrepancy8.10e-9 is below1e-7. This admits a geometric
proxy for these explicit cube scenes, not a new UE or physical sensor capture.

Each normalized arm retains the original hypothetical histogram model: .05m
bins, transitive .60m peak merging, .10m spread-to-MERGED rule, two-return cap,
per-zone normalized reflectance/range-squared strength, .01 floor, .04m Gaussian
noise and .02m quantization. Packet loss, observed IMU and frozen Radar are
unchanged. Different zone counts change deterministic RNG draw assignment;
this is a single fixed-seed comparison, not a confidence interval over noise.

Dense8 produces2771 VALID and1084 MERGED records; fine32 produces53828 VALID
and1239 MERGED. Counts have different numbers of zones and are not independent
obstacles. FP frames with a triggering MERGED return decline95 to62, while
VALID-trigger FP frames decline58 to53; these groups overlap. Remaining MERGED
full-range support, pose uncertainty and one-vote readout are still present.
These observations do not isolate any one of them as the sole remaining cause.

The stress arm scales fine-zone signal by1/16 before the unchanged detection
floor and assumes sigma .16m. **All returns disappear**, so the sigma assumption
has no measured downstream effect. The zero ToF FP is complete detection failure,
not precision improvement. This exposes dependence on the assumed measurement
budget, but is not a calibrated photon model or a prediction of any ST device.

## Hazard evidence and limits

Dense8 has detected contributions from the actual hazardous actor in110 true
frames; fine32 has123. Their ToF TP with no triggering hazardous-actor contribution
decrease20 to10. This improves native evidence coverage, while showing that even
fine32 alert totals include positives supported by other surfaces. All contributor
arrays remain evaluator-only and are saved separately from public observations.

The fine32 range/pose enclosure misses169 of455470 returned contributor samples;
dense8 misses0 of435771; legacy8 misses11 of27753. No angular clipping was applied.
These are observed enclosure mismatches, not proof that all intervals are valid:
Gaussian3-sigma bounds, finite within-return extent and fixed pose bounds are
assumptions. The result does not establish lossless physical localization. The
different sampling lattices between legacy8 and dense8 also prohibit treating
their sample-count gain as new real-world recall evidence.
