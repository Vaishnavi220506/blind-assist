## Three different failures, not one universal explanation

The missed HEAD event has no valid ToF targets in any of its 12 frames. Radar
returns on 10 frames, exactly the frames on which MZ116 alerts. Early's true
HEAD core cell scores only .135–.212; its maximum remains on a wrong far HEAD
cell, .171–.262. The two RGB previews inspected in this run show the suspended
object and thin rod/wall scene; those specific first-frame inherited visual
proposal lists are empty. This is evidence of available cues and missing branch
outputs, not proof of the internal learned failure cause or a sensor ceiling.

BODY differs: 221/222 changed native cells move in the correct score direction;
all 36 positive frame scores exceed all 36 negative scores, but both exceed
.58. Rod/farwall has only 107/216 changed cells moving in the correct direction,
with positive and negative scores heavily overlapping. A family-specific oracle
could distinguish BODY here, but family identity is evaluator metadata and is
not a permitted runtime threshold selector.

Exhaustive enumeration of the unchanged early scalar readout's 290 attainable
boundaries finds no point with both TP >= 139 and FP <= 116. Retaining 139 TP
requires at least 131 FP; allowing at most 116 FP permits at most 129 TP. This
narrows the original MZ123 conclusion for this checkpoint/readout on consumed
data. No threshold is selected, and no other architecture is ruled out.
