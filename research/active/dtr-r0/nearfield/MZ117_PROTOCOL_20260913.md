## Frozen method

Baseline: runnable MZ116 resolution_guard. Preserve all independently supported
Radar, its HEIGHT_UNKNOWN, optical range ages, and every valid ToF support.
Only SIM_MERGED components are eligible for the new conditional geometry.

Anchor selection: existing RGB proposals only. A SIM_VALID single-return zone
must lie wholly inside one box eroded2px, with no other proposal intersecting
its zone. At least6anchors, >=2rows and2columns; full-rank unit-ray design,
column-normalized condition<=100, maximum center-plane range residual<=.13m.
No image category, hidden sample ID or true pose is an input.

Camera-frame plane is beta·X=1. Solve beta_hat=pinv(U)*(1/r). Full finite-zone
unit-ray intervals give DeltaU; inverse-range uncertainty uses r±.13m, comprising
the existing3sigma=.12m plus half-quantization. Define A=abs(pinv(U))*DeltaU and
e=abs(pinv(U))*(inverse_range_error+DeltaU*abs(beta_hat)). If spectralradius(A)<1,
coefficient error d=(I−A)^−1e bounds common-plane substitution uncertainty.
Strictly positive full-ROI ray/plane denominator is required for inversion.
This is conditional on common surface and working noise bounds; Gaussian3sigma
is not an absolute bound, and the math does not prove object association.

Primary `surface`: a merged zone intersects exactly one qualifying visual box;
its reported mean±.13m must overlap the plane range interval. Restrict only to
zone intersect box padded2px. Propagate beta intervals and entire ROI rays,
then the existing pitch/yaw intervals to body XYZ. Missing/ambiguous/unbounded
fits preserve the original full-zone unknown range. Coarse certain-IN persists.

Diagnostic `surface_nominal_proxy`: same anchor selection, nominal fit and ROI,
but zero coefficient uncertainty and only.13m range padding. May use a
noncontractive fit. This explicitly weaker proxy tests the consequence of
discarding model uncertainty; it has no stronger authority than the primary.
