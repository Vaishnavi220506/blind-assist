# MZ165: pretrained context helps direct risk learning, with residual nuisance

EXPLORE consumed original TRAIN scene-heldout contrast, 2026-09-16.
[Protocol](MZ165_PROTOCOL_20260916.md).

Decision: **MZ165_PRETRAINED_CONTEXT_ALERT_GAIN_NOT_MET**. Frozen pretrained
DINOv2 context changes HELD random-control15TP/8FP/9FN to24/7/0; MZ129 is22/6/2.
Pretraining supplies a real matched learning benefit and recovers both incumbent
misses without losing or delaying old true warnings. However, all six baseline
boundary false frames remain and one new HEAD false frame appears. FIT accuracy
93.06% also misses the declared95%. Keep MZ129; stop the exact frozen context,
decoder, training and zero-cutoff recipe without threshold/epoch/size rescue.

Retain the measured pretraining effect for a distinct future task representation,
not this complete readout as an admitted alert replacement. This is stronger
evidence for direct risk features than MZ164's all-alert metric-depth output, but
the two are different experiments; only the random/pretrained comparison here
isolates the effect of pretrained weights. Neither natural scenes nor hardware
benefit is established, and the broad obstacle-improvement goal remains active.
