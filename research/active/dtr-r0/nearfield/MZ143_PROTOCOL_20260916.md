## Question

Can native-resolution RGB boundaries and complete regional ToF evidence become
useful corridor decisions when their metric relationship is explicit, without
learning a dense depth image or pooling away their positions?

MZ140's adapter omits both slots of multi-target zones and assigns accepted
regional ranges to zone-center pixels. MZ136's learned cell representation uses
mean/max pooled visual samples. MZ143 preserves all public slots, their angular
footprints, status and range uncertainty, and adds observable image-to-corridor
geometry hypotheses. Hypotheses are features, not certified surface enclosures.
MZ129 remains the comparator. No incumbent alarm is a predictor input or an OR
fallback. Missing/no-alert remains UNKNOWN.

  random_state 143016, n_jobs 4. Histogram boosting: 150 iterations, learning

  All feature extraction is untrained. No heldout fold labels train its model.
- Each operating threshold is the largest OOF score threshold retaining every
  baseline true-positive TRAIN frame; this also retains baseline event times.
  Candidate selection minimizes OOF false positives, then false segments, then
  prefers geometry ExtraTrees, sensor ExtraTrees, fused ExtraTrees, fused HGB.
- Refit each fixed model once on all TRAIN192, seal models, cutoffs and selected
  candidate before dev prediction. Dev predictions seal before dev truth parse.
  No dev threshold curves, hyperparameter search or second fit follows.
- Maximum run budget 20 minutes; no new capture, download, remote job or paid

## Research context

[DEPTHOR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Xiang_DEPTHOR_Depth_Enhancement_from_a_Practical_Light-Weight_dToF_Sensor_and_ICCV_2025_paper.html)
explicitly uses center-projected sparse depth without variance on ZJU-L5.
[SelfToF](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.13444v1) instead models zone-wise depth
consistency and scale recovery. These motivate preserving the sensor's regional
meaning; MZ143 implements neither paper and borrows no reported performance.
