# MZ159: observed reflection invariance

EXPLORE, 2026-09-16. Hypothesis: small asymmetric training panels allow a
classifier to depend on left/right coordinates even though the symmetric body
corridor label should not. Train on original and physically reflected observed
inputs, then average both view probabilities before the unchanged causal rule.
This changes training/readout symmetry, not thresholds on an old failed recipe.
MZ158 remains closed; its HEAD regression motivates broader transfer research.

## Fixed method and matched control

Inputs remain one RGB, original 8x8 two-return ToF, Radar and IMU. Reflect body y,
camera lateral offset and integrated yaw; pixel cx becomes width-1-cx; flip RGB
columns; reflect ToF zone columns and theta bounds while preserving target-slot
order; negate and re-sort the observed Radar range/angle/velocity tuples. Pitch,
range, radial velocity, masks and uncertainty remain unchanged. Missing or
discarded Radar returns are not reconstructed. This is an observed-input
transformation, not a new physical acquisition or independent sensor evidence.
Re-extract all 2485 MZ143 features because pixel proposal conventions do not
support a simple exact feature permutation. Preserve original public packets.

Candidate: reflection augmentation plus two-view mean probability. Matched
control: repeat each original twice in training, use original-view inference.
Both use the original HGB seed143016, 150 iterations, learning rate.05,
7 maximum leaves, L2=1, no early stopping; min_samples_leaf=16 instead of8
accounts for doubled view rows. Each view has weight.5, preserving total weight.
Mirrored views can fall into distinct leaves; doubled minimum row count does not
guarantee identical effective leaf regularization. The matched control exposes
sample-count/weight effects. Also retain unchanged MZ129 and MZ145 comparators.

Use only original TRAIN192 for fitting, with the original four scene folds.
Both views of a frame and its paired episode stay in the same fold. Average
candidate held-out view probabilities onto the original chronology. Derive low
and high using exactly MZ143 operating_threshold and MZ145 fit_onset on TRAIN
out-of-fold scores. Fit each final model once and seal models/cutoffs before
any dev prediction or evaluator parse. No grids, rescue selector or later refit.
