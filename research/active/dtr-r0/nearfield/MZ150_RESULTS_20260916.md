visible before dev, not only in its four HEAD false frames. A posthoc diagnostic
uses the unchanged final model once on its saved TRAIN features, without new
training, threshold selection, dev/test rescoring or a new candidate.

At the conventional .5 classifier boundary, used only to diagnose fit, the model
makes14 errors on1,344 fitted queries (717TP/11FP/3FN). Central fitted frames have

Central log loss rises from .07367 fitted to .37696 OOF. To retain all incumbent
OOF true frames, the frozen low threshold reaches .00608; that threshold creates
69 fitted and76 OOF single-frame false alerts. The observed fit/generalization
gap makes scene coverage and cross-scene score reliability a stronger next
diagnostic target than another temporal persistence rule. This does not prove
additional data alone will solve it, and .5 is not adopted as an alert threshold.
