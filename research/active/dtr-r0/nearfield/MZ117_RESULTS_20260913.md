## Why both plane arms did nothing

There are **1,168 merged components on 112 frames**. Every one fails the unique
qualified RGB-model eligibility requirement. Six models fit on six other frames,
all in `mz117_occluded_head` steps 00,01,04,08,09,10, each with eight anchors.
Those frames contain no merged targets. The fitted-model / merged-frame
intersection is therefore **zero**.

The six models also have noncontractive uncertainty matrices, spectral radii
1.4389–1.4723. But contraction is not the operative merged-target failure: no
merged component even reaches contraction, denominator or mean-overlap testing.
The nominal proxy therefore has zero coverage as well. No geometry was changed
and masked by another sensor's alert.

Both native contributor audits report zero exclusions because zero components
were refined. This is vacuous retention, not evidence that a successful plane
association would preserve a foreground rod. The fixed proposal/anchor recipe
fails operational coverage here; plane geometry in general has not been ruled out.
A future different mechanism needs observable support in actual mixed frames,
then a fresh complete scene test. Do not loosen this frozen recipe on these data.
